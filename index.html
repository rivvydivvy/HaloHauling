Who rents from us

Homeowners, contractors and businesses across the Bay Area rent our dumpsters. When a job has dirt and construction debris to clear, we set a box for each on the same driveway.

A dumpster rental in 3 steps

  1. 1. We deliver the dumpsterThe hook-lift sets it where you point, on a driveway or at the curb.
  2. 2. You fill the dumpsterLoad it at your own pace. Keep heavy material like concrete level with the rim.
  3. 3. We haul the load awayCall when it is full. We pick it up and take the load for disposal.
