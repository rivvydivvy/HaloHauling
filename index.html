04Materials

What goes In

Accepted

  • Construction and demolition debris
  • Dirt and concrete, in the 10-yard container
  • Mixed heavy debris, in the 10-yard container
  • Residential and commercial cleanout loads

Prohibited

Radioactive, volatile, highly flammable, explosive, biomedical, infectious, toxic or hazardous materials. For example:

  • Fluorescent lights
  • PCB ballasts
  • Oil filters
  • Acids
  • Ammunition
  • Anti-freeze
  • Asbestos
  • Batteries
  • Explosives
  • Gas tanks
  • Gasoline
  • Hazardous chemicals
  • Insecticides
  • Medical waste
  • Paint, stains and varnishes
  • Paint thinners and solvents
  • Pesticides
  • Garden chemicals
  • Poisons
  • Propane tanks
  • Motor oil

5-gallon barrels are accepted only when completely empty and open on both ends. Dry and fully drained paint cans are acceptable.

    Yes. We set several on one job, such as one for dirt and one for debris.

    On your driveway or on the street for residential jobs.

    Monday to Saturday, 6 am to 5 pm. We are closed on Sunday.
